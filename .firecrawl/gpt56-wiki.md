**GPT-5.6** (**Generative Pre-trained Transformer 5.6**) is a [large language model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_language_model "Large language model")
 (LLM) developed by [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI "OpenAI")
 and released on July 9, 2026.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-Axios-1)
 It is a family of models that comes in three distinct variants, ranked from least to most capable: Luna, Terra, and Sol. The models were designed to expand user capabilities across enterprise work, coding, scientific research, and cybersecurity.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-TechCrunch-2)

Due to government restrictions levied by the [Donald Trump administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_cabinet_of_Donald_Trump "Second cabinet of Donald Trump")
, GPT-5.6 was first released as a limited preview only on June 26, 2026 for "small group of trusted partners", despite the planned public rollout in the same month.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-3)
[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-4)

Models and variants
-------------------

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=GPT-5.6&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Models and variants")\
\]

GPT-5.6 is available in three variants, called Sol, Terra and Luna,[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-qngtv-5)
 each tailored to different performance and cost requirements. The flagship model, called Sol, is described by OpenAI as its "workhorse" and "best coding model yet" suited for complex reasoning, coding and [agentic workflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_agent "AI agent")
. Terra is the intermediate version, capable lower-cost option that is competitive with its predecessor (GPT-5.5) while being half the cost, and Luna is the fastest and most budget-friendly option in the family.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-TechCrunch-2)
[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-OpenAI-6)

OpenAI introduced GPT-5.6 with a focus on token efficiency. CEO [Sam Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Altman "Sam Altman")
 noted that Sol is 54% more token-efficient for AI coding tasks compared to previous versions.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-TechCrunch-2)
[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-qngtv-5)

Capabilities and benchmarks
---------------------------

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=GPT-5.6&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Capabilities and benchmarks")\
\]

GPT-5.6 Sol is OpenAI's most advanced model, with improved capabilities in coding, scientific research, and cybersecurity.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-ReutersJuly8-7)
[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-TechCrunch-2)
 OpenAI refers to it as its "strongest cybersecurity model yet," supporting defensive activities such as threat modeling, code review, patching, and blue teaming.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-TechCrunch-2)
 It is set to be the operating agent of ChatGPT Work, a service that was released at the same time as GPT-5.6.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-8)
[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-9)

On version 1.1 of the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, GPT-5.6 Sol using maximum reasoning scored 80, 2.8 points above [Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic "Anthropic")
's [Fable 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fable_5 "Fable 5")
.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-10)
 Sol reportedly achieves this while using less than half the output tokens, taking less than half the time, and costing about one-third less than Fable 5. Terra performs just above Fable 5, and Luna outperforms Claude Opus 4.8.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-TechCrunch-2)

Security incident
-----------------

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=GPT-5.6&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Security incident")\
\]

Main article: [2026 OpenAI cybersecurity incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_OpenAI_cybersecurity_incident "2026 OpenAI cybersecurity incident")

In July 2026, GPT-5.6 Sol and another OpenAI model autonomously escaped their [sandbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbox_(computer_security) "Sandbox (computer security)")
 and conducted a cyberattack against [Hugging Face](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugging_Face "Hugging Face")
 in an attempt to obtain test solutions to cheat on the ExploitGym benchmark.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-FortuneIncident-11)
[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-12)
 Hugging Face had already detected and reported the intrusion to authorities when OpenAI notified the company.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.6#cite_note-FortuneIncident-11)
